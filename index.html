Assessing the Impact of Public School Closure on Housing Markets in Michigan

Dissertation, Chapter 1 — to be completed August 2026

Examines whether public school closures in Michigan affected median home values between 2005 and 2019. Accounting for the systematic concentration of closures in larger, lower-income counties, a staggered difference-in-differences design shows that closures significantly reduce home values in the long run — driven by declines in population and housing demand.

Highway Length Matters: Unequal Racial Impacts of Interstate 10 in New Orleans

With Jhacova Williams & Bethany Perkins — R&R, Journal of Economic History

Using newly constructed data from historical maps, we exploit variation in interstate length within New Orleans neighborhoods to document the effect of Interstate 10 on housing outcomes. Black neighborhoods exposed to a longer span of the highway saw persistent declines in occupied housing units and home values; White neighborhoods saw no significant change.
